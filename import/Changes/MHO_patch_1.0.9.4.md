# Monster Hunter Outlanders – update 1.0.9.4 (data changes)

Resource version 1.0.9.4_4 (app 1.0.9.0), compared with 1.0.9.3_3. Extracted 2026-11-21 from the game files. Data tables (30 changed), English text (24 strings), assets (249 files). Compared 1.0.9.4 against the 1.0.9.3 state the game layers it on.

## Headlines

- Nergigante layered gear arrives: 10 armour pieces (α and β sets), 8 weapon skins and a Nergigante Style Crate.
- Rare, Epic and Superior Nergigante, Kushala Daora and Ruger Aidal Visages can now be bought in the Season Shop.
- Elder dragon event fights made easier: 15-minute time limit (was 10), Nergigante health −66%, Kushala Daora health −48%, lower recommended hunt power.
- Big health cuts (27–73%) for level 15–16 high-tier monsters, mostly Radiant.
- Adventurer Rank now gets much harder from AR 56 and stops at AR 60.
- Apex Challenge Orders now refill on Monday at 05:00 (was Friday).
- Freya's Festival Recruitment (from 27 Nov) gets its own pity counters; pity no longer carries over between Featured and Festival recruitments.

## New: Nergigante layered equipment

Season 1 adds Nergigante appearance (layered) gear. It changes looks only – it is not new armour or weapons with stats, so the weapon and armour lists are unchanged.

**Armour:** Nergigante Helm α, Nergigante Mail α, Nergigante Vambraces α, Nergigante Coil α, Nergigante Greaves α, Nergigante Helm β, Nergigante Mail β, Nergigante Vambraces β, Nergigante Coil β, Nergigante Greaves β

**Weapon skins:** Nergal Judicator (was Nergigante Great Sword), Nergal Reaver, Nergal Gouge (was Nergigante Dual Blades Model (Nergal Gouge)), Nergal Impaler, Nergal Roar (was Nergigante Heavy Bowgun Model (Nergal Roar)), Nergal Whisper (was Nergigante Bow Model (Nergigante Bow)), Nergal Crusher, Nergal Lacerator

**Renamed:** Annihilation Cannon → Destruction's Fusillade, Extermination's Claws → Perdition's Hand

**Nergigante Style Crate:** each armour piece 7%, each weapon skin 3.75%.

Each of the new Nergigante layered pieces gives 10 collection points (18 new CollectionScore rows). New 3D models for all 8 weapon skins and the α/β armour (male and female); 10 updated Nergigante armour icons, 1 new Charge Blade icon.


## Elder dragon Visages now obtainable

Rare, Epic and Superior Visages of Nergigante, Kushala Daora and Ruger Aidal were 'Not released'. They now list the Season Shop as their source, and their Visage Album entries now list all four qualities (before, the same Visage filled all four slots).

## Adventurer Rank: steep EXP from AR 56, cap at AR 60

The EXP value per Adventurer Rank is unchanged up to AR 55. From AR 56 it rises sharply, and AR 60 now needs 9,999,999 – a practical cap. Values above AR 60 are placeholders. The UI now shortens 'Adventurer Rank' to 'AR'.

| AR | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| 56 | 351,600 | 407,100 |
| 57 | 370,800 | 695,100 |
| 58 | 390,000 | 1,775,100 |
| 59 | 409,200 | 3,935,100 |

| 61 | 447,600 | 10,000,000 |
| 100 | 1,599,600 | 10,000,039 |
| 999 | 99,999,999 | 10,000,938 |

InvestigationProgressLevel grew from 37 to 153 levels (each 500 / 10000) – purpose unclear.


## Recruitment changes

- Crimson Authority – Freya (Festival Recruitment 3004, 27 Nov 2026 – 22 Jan 2027) now has its own SR (10) and SSR (90) pity counters instead of sharing the standard featured ones; featured pity stays 120.
- Rules text: SSR pity now carries over only to other recruitments of the same kind (Featured → Featured, Festival → Festival). Before, it carried over between Featured and Festival.
- Rates are now shown with four decimals (0.6000%, 50.0000%).

## Apex Challenge Orders refill day

3 Apex Challenge Orders now refill every Monday at 05:00 (was Friday), up to 6.


## Hunt list reward item

On 85 of the 236 hunt-list entries (all that used it), the reward item changed from Hunter's Jade (770002) to Adventurer's Hunting Insights (210002).


## New hunt entries

74 new hunt definitions. Player-facing ones: Kushala Daora (level 8), Ruger Aidal (level 14, two versions), Radiant Rathalos (levels 3, 5 and a level 10 Boss Hunt), Radiant Diablos (level 5), Radiant Pukei-Pukei (level 3), Rathian (level 7). The rest are test, training or single-monster level 1 entries. These match 5 new rows in the monster level list (Kushala Daora, Radiant Rathalos, Radiant Diablos, Ruger Aidal ×2).

An unnamed hunt for monster 140201 (internal name XingLong / 星龙, an elder dragon not in the game yet) – the same monster as one of the planned adventurers.


## Trial Expedition

- The Path of Expedition reset prompt was reworded and now warns if you still have uncleared Path of Trials stages, with a new 'Go to Path of Trials' button.
- New notice: 'New Path of Trials stage unlocked. Challenge now.'
- Bounty Trial rounds 5–8 now use different stages.

## Fixes and smaller changes

- Three Ruger Aidal field entries were tagged as Kushala Daora; now Ruger Aidal.
- Task fix: 'Break the throat of 7★+ Anjanath' → 'Break the head'. Three part-break tasks need fewer breaks (11 → 8, 8 → 6, 8 → 6).
- Photo task fix: 'Wigglers following the Wiggler Queen' → 'Marine Seahorses following the Marshal Seahorse'.
- Midori's Keepsake explanation text tweaked.
- Five preset trial loadouts (try-out adventurer builds) use lower gear levels (e.g. 12 → 10, 14 → 10).
- Heavy Bowgun: aerial shot and perfect-dodge attack hit setting changed (3 → 2); Cluster Ammo and Stylia's ultimate ability files updated. Barroth AI scripts updated.
- Wood, Ore and Plant Gatherer buildings: one value 40 → 20 (meaning unclear, possibly gather time).
- Resonance Crystal, Resonance Shard, the Engraved Gems, Radiant Gem/Crystal and Lucent Crystallite lost two item flags (effect unclear).
- 18 new Marathon Hunt loading screens. A reward preview no longer lists Guild Crimson Crest Charge Blade. One new shop link.
